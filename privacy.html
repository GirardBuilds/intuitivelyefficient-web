QuizChime Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

QuizChime is a flashcard app from Intuitively Efficient Software Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"). This page explains what the app does with information.

In short: QuizChime keeps your cards and study history on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sends your cards or study history to us or to anyone else.

1. What QuizChime stores on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your decks and cards, meaning the questions and answers you create or import;
  • your review history: when a reminder was sent, when you revealed a card and answered it, and how you graded it;
  • your scheduled reminder times and your settings, such as your daily rate, active hours and appearance.

We never receive this information and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device. See "Device backups" below for copies your device may keep.

2. What QuizChime does not do

  • It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • It does not collect device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution or payment libraries, and no third-party service receives data from it.
  • It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not use cookies.
  • We do not operate a server that the app connects to. On Android, the app does not hold the permission needed to access the internet at all.

3. What the app reads from your device

Beyond what you type in yourself, the app reads only:

  • your device's time zone, so reminders arrive in local time;
  • its own version number, which is shown in Settings;
  • whether notifications and exact alarms are currently allowed;
  • a CSV file, only when you choose to import one. You pick the file with your device's file picker, and the app reads it on your device and turns its rows into cards. It does not keep a copy of the file or its name.

4. Permissions

QuizChime asks for as few permissions as it can. Reminders are created on your device, and no server is involved.

  • Notifications (Android and iPhone): to show your reminders.
  • Exact alarms (Android): so a reminder arrives at its planned minute. If you decline, reminders can arrive later than planned.
  • Run at startup (Android): so reminders keep working after your device restarts.
  • Vibrate (Android): so a reminder can vibrate.

QuizChime does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and it can read only the file you choose when importing.

5. Reminders and your lock screen

Each reminder is one of two short sentences: "You have a question from" followed by the name of a deck, or "You're all caught up ✅". A card's question or answer never appears in a reminder, but the name of your deck does, and anyone who can see your lock screen can see it. Avoid putting sensitive information in deck names, or change your device's lock-screen notification settings.

6. Device backups

QuizChime never uploads your data. Your device's operating system, however, may include the app's data in backups it makes to your own account, such as Android's backup to your Google account, or iCloud and computer backups on iPhone, depending on your device settings. Those backups belong to you and are governed by Google's or Apple's own policies. We cannot see them. You can turn device backups off in your device settings. If you reinstall QuizChime or move to a new device, a backup may restore your cards and history.

7. Opening this page from the app

The "Privacy Policy" link in the app opens this page in your device's web browser. The link carries no information about you, and the app itself sends nothing. As with any website, the server that hosts this page can see the IP address and browser details of the request and may keep ordinary server logs. This page loads no scripts, trackers or third-party content of its own.

8. Security

Your data stays on your device, so your device's own protections, such as your screen lock and device encryption, are what protect it. We never receive a copy, so there is none on our side to lose or expose.

9. Children

QuizChime does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts and no features that connect to other people or to us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the date at the top.
